--- PLANT DISEASES AND TREATMENTS ---

Root Rot:
- Cause: Overwatering, poor drainage, fungal infection.
- Symptoms: Yellowing leaves, wilting despite wet soil, black mushy roots.
- Treatment: Remove plant from pot, cut off black roots, let dry, repot in fresh well-draining soil. Reduce watering.
- Prevention: Always use pots with drainage holes. Never let plants sit in standing water.

Powdery Mildew:
- Cause: Fungal disease, high humidity, poor air circulation.
- Symptoms: White powdery coating on leaves.
- Treatment: Spray with neem oil solution or baking soda mixture (1 tsp baking soda + 1 liter water).
- Prevention: Improve air circulation. Avoid overhead watering.

Leaf Spot (Black Spot / Brown Spot):
- Cause: Fungal or bacterial infection, overwatering, poor drainage.
- Symptoms: Brown or black spots on leaves, sometimes with yellow halo.
- Treatment: Remove affected leaves. Apply neem oil or copper fungicide.
- Prevention: Water at base, not on leaves. Ensure good drainage.

Aphids:
- Cause: Small sap-sucking insects, usually green, black, or white.
- Symptoms: Curled or distorted leaves, sticky residue (honeydew) on leaves.
- Treatment: Spray with neem oil solution or insecticidal soap. Blast with water.
- Prevention: Inspect plants regularly. Introduce ladybugs as natural predators.

Spider Mites:
- Cause: Tiny spider-like pests, thrive in hot dry conditions.
- Symptoms: Fine webbing on leaves, tiny dots on leaf surface, yellowing leaves.
- Treatment: Spray with neem oil or miticide. Increase humidity.
- Prevention: Keep plants well-watered. Mist leaves regularly.

Whiteflies:
- Cause: Small white flying insects that suck plant sap.
- Symptoms: Yellowing leaves, white flies when plant is disturbed, sticky leaves.
- Treatment: Yellow sticky traps. Neem oil spray. Insecticidal soap.

Overwatering Signs:
- Yellow leaves, mushy stems, mold on soil surface, root rot, wilting despite wet soil.
- Fix: Reduce watering frequency. Improve drainage. Repot if necessary.

Underwatering Signs:
- Dry crispy leaf edges, drooping wilted leaves, dry soil pulling away from pot edges.
- Fix: Water thoroughly until water drains from bottom. Increase watering frequency.

--- GENERAL PLANT CARE TIPS ---

Watering Tips:
- Always check soil moisture before watering. Stick finger 1-2 inches into soil.
- Water in the morning to allow leaves to dry during the day.
- Use room temperature water. Cold water can shock tropical plants.
- Rainwater or filtered water is better than tap water for sensitive plants.
- Pots must have drainage holes to prevent root rot.

Fertilizing Tips:
- Fertilize during growing season (spring and summer). Reduce or stop in winter.
- Never fertilize a stressed or sick plant.
- Always water before fertilizing to avoid burning roots.
- Yellow leaves can indicate nitrogen deficiency.
- Purple leaves can indicate phosphorus deficiency.

Repotting Tips:
- Repot when roots come out of drainage holes or plant becomes root-bound.
- Best time to repot is spring.
- Choose a pot only 1-2 inches larger than current pot.
- Use fresh potting mix when repotting.

Light Tips:
- Bright indirect light: Near window but not in direct sun beam.
- Direct light: Full sun on windowsill or outdoors.
- Low light: Away from windows, shaded areas.
- Rotate plants every few weeks for even growth.

Humidity Tips:
- Most tropical plants prefer 40-60% humidity.
- Increase humidity by misting leaves, using a pebble tray with water, or a humidifier.
- Group plants together to create a humid microclimate.

Propagation Tips:
- Stem cuttings: Cut below a node, remove lower leaves, place in water or soil.
- Leaf cuttings: Used for succulents and snake plants.
- Division: Separate root clumps when repotting.
- Best time to propagate is spring and summer.

--- SEASONAL PLANT CARE IN PAKISTAN ---

Summer (April - September):
- Water more frequently due to heat.
- Move sensitive plants away from harsh afternoon sun.
- Watch for spider mites and aphids which thrive in heat.
- Good plants to grow: Hibiscus, Marigold, Chillies, Tomatoes.

Winter (October - March):
- Reduce watering for most plants.
- Bring tropical plants indoors.
- Good plants to grow: Spinach, Coriander, Poinsettia, Lavender.
- Protect plants from frost in northern Pakistan.

Monsoon Season:
- Reduce watering as rain provides moisture.
- Watch for fungal diseases due to high humidity.
- Ensure good drainage to prevent waterlogging.
- Apply neem oil to prevent fungal and pest issues.
